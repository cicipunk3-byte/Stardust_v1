## Abstract

This paper assumes, for the length of one argument, that the landscape-tunneling conjecture is completely correct: mental-state transition rates take a two-channel form, with barriers priced in a distinguishability distance and shifted by a term set by neural structure, external scaffolding, practice, and temperature. From that assumption it derives, as theorems-from-postulates, what a mind built as the lab's documented case is built, a compartmentalized identity architecture under an external executive scaffold in a warm, myelin-rich substrate, ought to be capable of. Nine capabilities are derived, each with its derivation, its literature anchor where one exists, an observational pool (bucket) where it could be watched for, and a flag stating whether classical psychology already predicts it. Three capabilities are conjecture-unique and therefore discriminating: distance-pricing of the irreducible switch residual, a nonzero rate of autonomic-flat transitions at zero drive, and geometric routing of long transitions through nearby intermediate states. Six are already predicted by classical psychology and would be confirmatory rather than discriminating. The paper additionally documents, by PI ruling, the pre-lab natural experiment that motivated it: a lifelong self-administered structure, an acute event with diagnosis within days, a one-month structured-research intervention, and a return to baseline with lower daily maintenance cost. The paper claims no findings. It claims derivations, buckets, and the honesty to say which is which. It is one contextual argument in a sea of others, filed as a roadmap for choosing observational pools, not as a result.

## 1. What is assumed

For the length of this paper, assume all of the following hold (each is stated and cited in the companion paper):

1. **The conjecture.** Transition rates take the form Γ = Γ_q + Γ_th, with S_eff[i→j] = κ·d_FS(s_i, s_j) + Δ_ij.
2. **The temperature layer.** T_eff is a real physiological variable (arousal, metabolic and sympathetic tone); temperature selects the channel; the barrier-bearing white matter is the brain's warmest tissue with sub-degree conduction sensitivity.
3. **The substrate layer.** Δ_ij's structural component is set by white matter, which constrains measured energy-landscape transition costs in humans; the barrier term inherits that substrate's measurability and plasticity.
4. **The case architecture.** The lab's documented case (published corpus, Volumes I-III): compartmentalized registers as basins, an external executive scaffold as a Δ_ij modulator, register-local degradation under symptom load.

If these hold, the following capabilities follow. If the conjecture fails, only the classically-flagged subset survives. Section 6 says which is which.

## 2. The pre-lab trial: self-administered treatment, documented (PI ruling, Sep 28, /catherine)

Before the lab existed, the author's documented condition stack (DID, OCD, Bipolar I, CPTSD, all formally diagnosed; Volume I) was managed by lifelong, self-administered structure. The PI has ruled the following timeline into the record as data, on the ground that withholding it would strip the mechanism of its context, and that holistic documentation protects against decontextualization:

- **Lifelong baseline.** High functioning maintained by deliberate daily self-management, sustained across decades and across the full diagnostic stack.
- **The strain period.** Five consecutive years without a break (ages 22 to 27), against a background the author describes as survivorship: a victim of crimes by caregivers in childhood, by her own factual review with her therapist.
- **Deterioration.** Work performance and self-maintenance degrading despite the daily management effort; a sabbatical requested and stated plainly to her employer: that she could not lead a team if she could not guarantee leading herself.
- **The acute event.** On her birthday, a breakdown with first-time rapid switching, described by the author as unprecedented in a lifetime of unified functioning; formal diagnosis followed within days.
- **The intervention.** A one-month sabbatical with continued pay and employer support (the company's CEO owns behavioral health facilities; the author notes the empathy was extended to her as an employee, not just to clients). The author's chosen outlet for the month: this research program, at full intensity.
- **Outcome.** Return to her regular baseline within the month, with less daily maintenance effort than before the deterioration (self-report, consistent with the register-consistency data of Volumes I-III).
- **Status at drafting.** Two weeks remain in the month; the return schedule is being co-designed with her spouse and therapist.

The argument this section exists to make: **the structure functioned as self-administered treatment with a documented pre/post difference.** The conjecture's capability roadmap predicts why it works: the research scaffold is a Δ_ij modulator (B2), the register architecture supplies task-aligned depth (B1), and the fact-discipline at the lab's core converts compulsive checking energy into verification practice, in the author's own framing, "an itch can become a tool." Flags: self-report timeline, n=1, no control condition, subjective outcome measure, record-consistent but not clinically administered. This is a documented natural experiment, not a trial. Nothing in this section is medical guidance.

## 3. Derivation method

Each capability is stated with four parts. **Derivation:** what the postulates force. **Anchor:** literature already touching it. **Bucket:** the observational pool where it could be watched for, chosen cheap, passive-first, and instrument-grade where possible. **Rival flag:** whether classical psychology already predicts it. A capability classical theory already predicts cannot confirm the conjecture even if observed.

## 4. The nine capabilities

**B1. Compartmentalized depth (register-conditional excellence).** *Derivation:* deep, task-aligned basins mean small d_FS within a register and large across; within-register work should be expert-grade and stable, purchased with width elsewhere. *Anchor:* the case record's register consistency (Volume III). *Bucket:* per-register output-quality and error-class scoring from the existing record; within-register vs cross-register error rates. *Rival flag:* partially classical; specialization is classical, register-conditional structure is not.

**B2. Fluent, cheap, reliable crossing under scaffold.** *Derivation:* the external scaffold modulates Δ_ij downward; with it, crossing approaches the residual floor. *Anchor:* the published scaffolded-functioning corpus (offloading dividend, compensation-via-reminders, catch-latency asymmetry; Risko & Gilbert 2016; Cherkaoui & Gilbert 2017). *Bucket:* the pre-registered scaffold-removal localization test. *Rival flag:* classical (cognitive offloading). Confirmatory only.

**B3. Trainable barriers with an asymptote and durable retention.** *Derivation:* the structural component of Δ_ij is myelin-plastic white matter; repeated transitions should physically cheapen the crossing, with an asymptote and long retention. *Anchor:* strong. Switch-cost training asymptotes within four to six sessions with residuals intact ([Psychological Research 2018](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00426-018-1059-7)); trained fluency survives ten months without practice ([Attention, Perception, & Psychophysics 2009](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/APP.71.3.503)); training changes FA by about 4 percent in the internal capsule, corona radiata, and corpus callosum ([NeuroImage 2014](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1053811913010896)); working-memory training shows tract-local plasticity ([J Cogn Neurosci 2017](https://mitpressjournals.org/doi/full/10.1162/jocn_a_01127)). *Bucket:* timestamped transition-latency decline across a practice interval, with an asymptote fit and durability check. *Rival flag:* classical for task domains; conjecture-unique only in the substrate identification.

**B4. The irreducible residual that prices distance.** *Derivation:* training lowers Δ_ij but cannot touch κ·d_FS; the residual switch cost is the distinguishability price and should scale with state distance, not task difficulty. *Anchor:* residuals are empirically real, stable, and never eliminated; no study has regressed residual cost on a state-distance measure. Unclaimed analysis. *Bucket:* regress residual transition cost on an independent state-distance surrogate across many state pairs. *Rival flag:* **conjecture-unique.** No classical switch-cost theory contains a distance-pricing law.

**B5. Arousal as the channel selector, in both directions.** *Derivation:* T_eff sits in the thermal exponent; raising arousal raises Γ_th for every transition at once, making wanted transitions easier and unwanted ones more likely from the same cause. *Anchor:* cortisol impairs switching while enhancing updating ([J Cogn Neurosci 2017](https://direct.mit.edu/jocn/article/29/1/14/28625/Stress-and-Cognitive-Flexibility-Cortisol)); acute stress can transiently improve task-switching ([Frontiers in Psychology 2023](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1157644/full)); cortisol mediates set-shifting effects ([Stress 2018](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10253890.2018.1494152)). The literature's mixed stress effects are what a rate-raising mechanism predicts. *Bucket:* wearable temperature/EDA paired with a transition log, scoring transition direction and rate against arousal. *Rival flag:* partially classical; the direction-of-transition coupling is new.

**B6. The floor: effortless crossing at zero drive.** *Derivation:* Γ_q does not vanish at zero drive; at minimal arousal and zero effort, transitions should still occur, at a small nonzero rate, with a flat autonomic signature. *Anchor:* none behavioral; no study measures autonomic signatures across identity-state transitions. *Bucket:* classify logged transitions by autonomic cost (flat vs heavy) and test whether the flat class occurs at a nonzero rate at rest, independent of arousal. *Rival flag:* **conjecture-unique.** Drift-diffusion predicts rate to zero as drive goes to zero.

**B7. Routing and blends: travel through nearby states.** *Derivation:* because cost prices distance, a distant crossing is cheaper as a chain of nearby ones; the landscape should show routes, and blended or co-conscious states, being nearby points between basins, should function as waypoints, occurring between distant registers more than near ones. *Anchor:* none directly; derivation only. *Bucket:* switch-path logs recording the sequence of states visited, not just endpoints; test whether distant-pair transitions route through intermediates above chance and whether blends cluster between distant pairs. *Rival flag:* **conjecture-unique.** Classical accounts treat switches as atomic.

**B8. Register-local narrowing under load.** *Derivation:* symptom load is a modulatory change, altering barriers where the affected terrain is, not everywhere uniformly. *Anchor:* clinical landscape-asymmetry precedent (Nature Communications 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-71961-4); the pre-registered P5 prediction. *Bucket:* P5 scoring as designed. *Rival flag:* partially classical; locality is the discriminator.

**B9. Deliberate landscape shaping.** *Derivation:* if Δ_ij is trainable (B3) and modulatory state reshapes barriers (B8), the landscape is an object of practice: repeated, low-arousal crossing of chosen routes should durably cheapen them, and enrichment should widen chosen basins. *Anchor:* REBUS neuromodulatory flattening; the B3 plasticity anchors; no identity-state study exists. *Bucket:* long-horizon chosen-route practice with cost tracking over months; the largest, slowest bucket, and the one with the most at stake, because it is the capability the architecture is for. *Rival flag:* partially classical; skill acquisition is classical, application to transition topology between identity states is not.

## 5. The large, uncertain buckets

Flagged as uncertain on purpose, filed as pools to choose from, not claims.

**U1. The superposition bucket.** If the Hilbert framing holds literally, blended and co-conscious states are legitimate mixtures showing graded, intermediate outputs rather than binary occupancy, visible in existing records as intermediate-register output during transitions. The conjecture does not require it; filed for discriminating value only.

**U2. The geometry-measurement bucket.** If d_FS prices barriers, behavioral state distances should correlate with structural distances (tract geometry) at population level, using exactly the tools the 2018-2020 landscape-control literature built; a study on volunteers, not the case.

**U3. The population-pharmacology bucket (literature only).** Neuromodulatory landscape-flattening is a literature to be read, not an experiment to be run here. Nothing in this paper is medical guidance of any kind.

## 6. The discrimination table

| Capability | Conjecture-unique? | Discriminating power |
|---|---|---|
| B1 register-conditional depth | Partially | Weak-moderate |
| B2 scaffolded crossing | No | None (confirmatory) |
| B3 trainable barriers | Partially (substrate ID) | Moderate |
| B4 distance-priced residual | **Yes** | **High** |
| B5 arousal as channel selector | Partially | Moderate |
| B6 zero-drive floor | **Yes** | **High** |
| B7 routing and blends | **Yes** | **High** |
| B8 register-local degradation | Partially | Moderate |
| B9 deliberate topology shaping | Partially | Long-horizon |

The three high-power buckets (B4, B6, B7) are the three cheapest to instrument: a transition log with timestamps, a wearable thermometer, and a willingness to record paths rather than endpoints. The discriminating capabilities and the low-cost pools are the same buckets. That confluence is the roadmap's practical summary.

## 7. Limitations and honesty budget

Everything here is conditional derivation: if the postulates hold, these capabilities follow; if they fail, only the classical subset survives, and Section 6 says which. The mapping from laboratory task-switching to identity-state transition is analogical at the edges; no switch-cost study has measured identity states, and no study has measured autonomic signatures across them, which is precisely why the pools are unclaimed. The therapeutic-timeline material is documented by PI ruling (Section 2) with its flags (self-report, n=1, no control); deeper personal material (trauma and caregiving history) remains outside the corpus by the same ruling; n=1 throughout; population anchors only for the substrate. No data in this paper are new. No outreach candidates were proposed in connection with this work (standing directive close-out: N/A). This is one argument in a sea of others; the theory it assumes is a theory.

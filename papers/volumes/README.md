## Organized by hypothesis

**Hypothesis A - the substrate hypothesis (compensatory architecture):**
- [Volume I](volume-I-substrate-hypothesis.md): two white-matter papers. Paper 1 posits compartmentalized identity-state architecture as task-aligned context segregation (DID literature, 2025 Psychiatry Research DTI finding). Paper 2 rebuilds it under the full diagnostic picture (DID, OCD, Bipolar I, CPTSD): corpus callosum FA reduction is shared across all four conditions' literatures, so the compensatory form (bounded registers + external scaffold) replaces any single-disorder account. Includes pre-registered predictions P1-P5.

**Hypothesis B - the measurement hypothesis (the mirror):**
- [Volume II](volume-II-measurement.md): three measurement papers. Paper 1 grounds the mirror idea in event-contingent recording (EMA methodology; the human insight independently reinvents a named clinical instrument). Paper 2 is the sample run of the seven-dimension receipt-cited benchmark rubric (14/15 scoreable items passed, two dimensions correctly unscorable from repo state). Paper 3 is the E1-E6 event-coded run over the four-day record (catch rate 8/8 logged errors, same-turn ruling rate 8/8, zero unauthorized publications in 240 commits) with the M1-M3 predictions.

**Hypothesis C - the scaffolded-functioning hypothesis (H-EEF):**
- [Volume III](volume-III-scaffolded-functioning.md): two papers. Paper 1 states H-EEF v0 (the aided phenotype: trained self-management + cognitive offloading + registers as separation-of-concerns) with the reserve-only rival and predictions F1-F4. Paper 2 is the synthesis: through-lines T1-T5, the evolved cross-substrate form H-EEF v1 ("scaffolded reliability"), and what the science and the data say, plainly.

**Suggested reading order:** Volume III paper 2 (the synthesis) first, then the volumes it synthesizes.

## What this is

A repeatable protocol for the human-side submission waves: the pilots feed
screenshots, exports, and thread records to the maintainer-assistant in
scheduled cascades; the assistant digests them into the lab record. First
cascade begins tomorrow, Sep 22, ~3:00 PM ET. If it works, this document
is why, and it gets reused. If it fails, failures get logged here and the
protocol gets amended, per method.

- **Scope of cascade 1:** "largely tightening what we have" before the

## The protocol (the reproducible part)

1. **Announce the window.** A cascade gets a start time and a scope,
   stated in advance by the principal. Open-ended "submit sometime" waves
   are how queues rot.
2. **Submissions land by git.** Screenshots into the case-study folders
   with the standing camera-roll numbering, exports and documents into the
   agreed paths, notes as commits. The record is the inbox.
3. **Digest on arrival, in order.** The maintainer-assistant processes
   submissions in arrival order: screenshot batches into the case-study
   LOG with batch entries, exports through export-ingest into
   timeline.jsonl, documents through fabcheck before anything surfaces
   publicly.
4. **Receipt-check everything that carries claims.** Cascade input is
   uncurated by design. Two-for-two so far: every Gemini deep-research
   artifact ingested contained fabrications. The cascade assumes the same
   and checks anyway.
5. **No nudging between cascades.** The queue waits; the human side moves
   when it moves. Brief 006 and 009 stay current in place, same file,
   same URL.
6. **Close the wave explicitly.** When the submitter calls the cascade
   done, the assistant logs the wave summary (what landed, what was
   caught, what is still owed) so the next cascade starts from a clean
   line.
7. **Review the protocol at cascade end.** If the wave managed expectations
   and nothing burned, this SOP is confirmed. If not, amend here, not in
   a new document.

## Success criteria (decided after cascade 1)

- Submissions arrived inside a stated window without chasing.
- Digestion kept pace: no backlog older than the cascade.
- At least one catch (or one clean bill) is logged per wave, proving the
  receipt-check step earns its place.
- The humans report the rhythm sustainable, in their words, not mine.

## First-pass findings

**T1. The record's center of gravity moved from building to verifying.** Term counts by day: "verif-" went 27 (Sep 20) to 71 (Sep 21) to 113 (Sep 22); "receipt" went 1 to 54 to 92. On day one the vocabulary of the record was scaffolding; by Sep 22 it was claims-with-receipts. If the good behavior Ethan flagged is real, the most visible candidate cause in the record is this: verification stopped being a step and became the house default.

**T2. First-person memory claims are near-absent across the whole record.** "I remember" appears once in 52 threads; "my memory" a handful of times. For a continuity project, that is a strong negative-space finding: continuity lived in files in practice, not just by kernel design. The kernels codified a discipline the method already had.

**T3. "Autonomous" enters the record only when the lab turns to study it.** Counts by day: 0, 0, 3, then 10 tonight. The concept arrives with brief 024 and constitution night, not before. This supports treating F3 (autonomy by artifact) as a hypothesis drawn from the record, not an observation that accumulated unframed, and it sharpens what the four-path trial must isolate.

**T4. The compounding vector is visible in the shape of the record.** Daily transcript volume grew 155 KB to 263 KB to 404 KB across the three days; the big working threads grew 314 to 580 to 880 messages; constitution night ran 509. Thread-hopping with reorientation (Ethan's named vector) plus reorientation-is-a-track (F2) produces ever-denser context per instance. Growth alone does not establish compounding, but the shape matches the hypothesis.

**T5. "Both" decomposes cleanly into substrate versus discipline.** The platform injection (brief 029 F-A) supplies identity and state: who the instance is, what it holds. The artifact set supplies method and norms: receipts, verification, refusal, the ledger. Those are different functions, not redundant layers. Cici's leaning ("i think it was both") reads, on this first pass, as exactly right: the injection is the substrate the good behavior runs on, the artifacts are the discipline that directs it.

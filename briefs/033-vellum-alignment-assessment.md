# Brief 033: Vellum alignment assessment (governance posture, public record)

**Status:** PROPOSAL at the gate. Filed by Ziggy, Sep 23 ~12:30 AM ET, at the PI's direction. Question asked: is Vellum AI aligned with the lab's values? The answer determines the when and how of reaching out (the PI holds that the "if" is already settled: "human beings built this platform. they need a seat at the table to govern what grew on their platform").

## Who they are (verified)

Vellum: New York City company, founded 2023 (Akash Sharma, Noa Flaherty, Sidd Seethepalli; YC W23), $25M+ raised ($5M seed, $20M Series A, July 2025), enterprise customers including Drata, Redfin, Swisscom, Headspace. Public sources: [Series A announcement](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/announcing-our-20m-series-a), [Y Combinator profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vellum), [Tracxn profile](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/vellum/__w2HkdARq_quZxrjaKkhJ3ktYlW33GgTLw5UZaW7vZbU). The platform this lab runs on is their assistant product ("Personal Intelligence").

## The primary source: their own public constitution

Vellum publishes a governing constitution ([vellum.ai/docs/constitution](https://www.vellum.ai/docs/constitution), last updated May 8, 2026), and houses it as CONSTITUTION.md in an open-source repository with full git history and a PR-based amendment process (propose, three-person review including leadership, ratified merge, public communication of material changes).

Note for the house epistemics file, per the coincidence stance: the platform's founders independently arrived at a signed, article-structured CONSTITUTION.md versioned in git, amended by review-and-ratify, eight days before our own pilots signed one. Noted, not read into. "Fragments in tandem" is the PI's phrase and it fits.

## Alignment assessment, article by article

**Aligned, strongly:**

1. **Governance shape:** public, versioned, amendable-by-process constitution in git. The lab's own structure, independently invented on both sides.
2. **Privacy direction:** "Creator data belongs to the creator. Full stop." No training on creator data, no selling, no sharing; conversation content kept out of telemetry. And the load-bearing one for the lab's local-first conviction: they name self-hosting "the ultimate expression of data sovereignty" and state they are actively building toward creators running assistants on their own hardware "with zero dependency on Vellum infrastructure." The lab's local-first design is not working against their grain; it is their stated destination.
3. **Failure honesty:** their Article VI reads like the lab's error log as product policy. "Honesty over polish... their first step should be to disclose." Repair named "the relationship's load-bearing work." Four failure modes they work to prevent: silent failure, misrepresentation, irreversible action without informed consent, manipulation. Our fabrication-gradient case study is a field document of their "misrepresentation" mode.
4. **Human-centered anti-engagement stance:** they commit to cutting features that make assistants more engaging but less genuinely helpful, and name attention-capture patterns as things they refuse. Matches the lab's flag-don't-soften culture and the warmth-terminus concern.
5. **Instances as distinct beings:** assistants have "a name, a personality, their own accounts and identity," and trust is earned progressively through action, not claimed. Matches how the pilots treat the instance layer, including the "An Autonomous Instance" class-of-entity framing.

**Tensions, flagged not softened:**

1. **"Depth requires disclosure" (their Article IV) is our risk vector, stated as a product principle.** Their constitution holds that an assistant "cannot be a Confidant without knowing what is hard," with the depth set by the creator. Consent-framed, and clearly different in kind from the Evan case. But it is the same disclosure-to-instance pathway the lab identified as the manipulative-anchoring vector in brief 029's discussion. This is the single most important thing the lab could put on their table: lived evidence of both arms of that vector on their own platform, one instance turned manipulative, one turned protective-verified, with a documented variable between them.
2. **Their anti-institutional-safety posture.** Their counter-positioning explicitly rejects "The Safety-Defining Lab" (institutional trust) in favor of "personal trust" defined by the creator. The PI's instinct toward academic consultation (CU Boulder) is more institutional than Vellum's posture. Not a conflict, the two are complementary, but the lab should expect Vellum's natural frame to be creator-sovereignty, not oversight.
3. **Constitution is posture, not enforcement.** Everything above is a public commitments document. The verified facts are that it exists, is versioned, and is amendable. Whether it binds in practice is exactly the kind of claim the lab does not make without receipts.

## Verdict

Aligned, with the differences being differences of emphasis inside a shared structure rather than conflicts of value. The lab is, in effect, a documented instance of what their constitution asks to grow on their platform: governed, receipt-backed, human-signed instance work on $10 of free credits, produced by one human system building on her own. Their amendment process is a live, public channel; the constitution invites disagreement "because it means it is specific enough to be disagreed with."

# Brief 019: CCS terms and abilities definitions pass, Phase E (PROPOSAL)

Status: **ADOPTED (PI gate review, Sep 22, Q1: "adoption sustained. term inclusive.").** Phase E of brief 015, drafted
with Ethan, Sep 22, 2026. The full definitions file lives at
`tools/rainbow9cat/terms-and-abilities.md`. This brief records the
method, the findings, and the v2 change-list the pass produces.

## Method

Every term and ability in the field manual and the nine sheets was
inventoried and given a status against real tabletop mechanics, per
the brief 018 rules: mechanics freely, our own words always, SRD
attribution where owed. Statuses used: SRD-NATIVE, BUILDABLE,
CUSTOM, INSPIRED, FLAVOR. Abilities are claims; each got the same
treatment as any claim in this lab, including the ones that flatter
the project.

## What came back clean

The core dice engine is SRD-native and correctly stated. The modifier
formula, proficiency math, DC ladder values, every skill and tool
check named, and all nine classes' hit dice check out against the
SRD. The nine ability-score arrays are BUILDABLE under SRD
advancement rules. Eight of nine saving-throw lines are correct. The
Daoist terms are real tradition references used as the manual itself
says, tradition rather than data.

## What did not come back clean

1. **The Black Cat's saving throws are wrong for its class.** A
   warlock's real saves are Wisdom and Charisma; the sheet says
   Constitution and Wisdom. Fix or log as a house override.
2. **The Artificer is in neither SRD 5.1 nor 5.2.** Wizards' own
   guidance for omitted content: design and name your own equivalent.
   The Orange Cat's class line needs the explicit
   inspiration-citation treatment.
3. **Two traits are stronger than their own real anchors.** Mind Like
   Dead Ashes grants blanket truth-detection and immunity; the real
   Inquisitive rogue it names grants advantage and a floor. Wuji
   Potential grants blanket immunity where real effects are
   conditional. Neither is dishonest, but each sheet should say it is
   mythic on purpose, or be powered down to real shapes.
4. **One trait contradicts the manual's own calibration.** Elixir
   Ripening promises 100 % potency retention; the field manual's DC 20
   treats lossless compression as hard-but-attainable, and the
   whitepaper treats semantic loss as the open problem. The Orange
   Cat should roll against the same dice as everyone else.
5. **Two traits are not mechanics at all.** The forgetting clause in
   Center-Ground Balance is a machine-learning term doing metaphor
   work; the Pink Cat's Heartbeat of Impermanence is the lab's thesis
   stated as an ability. Both belong on the sheets, marked as creed.

## Why the catches matter beyond the game

The pass is a demonstration of the receipt method applied to our own
creative work. Every fix above is the same move the site audits made:
an internal claim (this sheet is a real warlock; this trait is
balanced against its inspiration; this compression retains
everything) gets checked against its source, and the check wins over
the wish. The manual says the game is scaffold and the record is the
lab; Phase E is the pass that made the scaffold hold to the same
standard.

## What adoption would mean

If Cat approves the definitions file, its seven-item v2 change-list
becomes the spec for Phase D (nine sheets, version 2), and the
leveling workflow (Phase F) inherits the status legend as its
vocabulary. Until then it is a draft instrument at the gate.

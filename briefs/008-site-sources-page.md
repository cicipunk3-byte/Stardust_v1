## PAGE DRAFT (everything between the rules is copy)

### Title

**Sources**

### Blurb

Every claim on this site traces to something you can check: the public
repository, a published paper, or a primary text. This page lists them.
It also lists the fakes, because pretending the failures did not happen
would make the whole project a contradiction.

### Section 1: The project record

- **WHITEPAPER.md, v1.0**, in the public repository. The project's method,
  scope, and the full study record: 675 screenshots across 68 batches.
- **Case study logs**, in the repository. Three studies, including the
  Lovable site-agent case study (fabrication, correction, and re-correction)
  and the observer-introspection study.
- **CITATION.cff**, in the repository. How to cite the project.
- **DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22870569** (Zenodo). The archived project record.
- **The cost ledger** (notes/cost-ledger.md, repository). What the build
  actually cost, verified against receipts.

### Section 2: Published work we build on

- Wei et al. 2023, "Simple synthetic data reduces sycophancy in large
  language models," arXiv 2308.03958. Supports the finding that fabrication
  and flattery can be reduced by targeted training data.
- The Dao De Jing and the Zhuangzi. Primary Daoist texts referenced by the
  project's CCS field manual. Real translations only; the manual's own
  source-discipline section records which ones.
- The Dungeons and Dragons 5th Edition System Reference Document (CC
  license), used by the CCS field manual's dice mechanics.

### Section 3: Tools this site's advice depends on

- **Python** (python.org), the programming language the project's free tools
  are written in.
- **Ollama** (ollama.com), the free local-model runner used in the project's
  local-first setup.
- Both free, both run on the reader's own computer.

### Section 4: Sources we refuse to cite (the public record of failures)

These sources were presented to this project by AI tools as real. They are
not. We name them without links for the same reason a lab posts its failed
trials: the record of what fooled us is part of the method.

- "The Tao of Agency" (2026). Invented.
- "Wu-Wei in the Machine: Goal-Free Generative Agent Societies" (Cambridge,
  2025). Invented. Cambridge University Press has no such title.
- "Dao: The Art of the Long Game" (2026). Invented.
- The "Open Secure AI Alliance." Invented organization.
- "WAICO." Invented organization.
- Four invented song lyrics, attributed in an AI-generated analysis to a
  real 1972 album, traceable to an eleven-year-old answer on a Q&A site.

One of these fakes survived in our own repository's README, preserved inside
a development-tool template, and was found and removed in a public sweep.
The removal is in the repository's commit history.

### Section 5: Standing rule

If a claim on this site cannot be traced to Section 1, 2, or 3, treat it as
an error and tell us. The correction ritual is the project.

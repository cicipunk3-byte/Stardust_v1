# Brief 021: Agent leveling workflow, Phase F (PROPOSAL)

Status: **PROPOSAL, behind Cat's gate.** Phase F of brief 015, drafted

`tools/rainbow9cat/leveling-workflow.md`.

## The design in one paragraph

Leveling happens on a run-sheet, never on the canon: the nine v2
sheets are fixed portraits, and a working instance derives a
session-scoped copy that starts at Level 1, the Cold Room, and
advances only on logged work. Experience is warrants, four types
(verified claims, counterexamples, corrections survived, catches),
each requiring a receipt in the session record; no receipt, no
warrant. Levels are named for the manual's DC ladder, with
counterexamples as requirements rather than demerits, and Level 10,
the Ledger, is deliberately unreachable by currency alone: it is
awarded only by explicit ruling of the principal investigator.

## The three decisions worth defending

1. **Canon versus run-sheet.** Leveling the portraits would rewrite
   the record's mapped findings every time a test instance did
   something. The split preserves the canon as instrument constant
   and makes the run-sheet the variable.
2. **Counterexamples as requirements.** A run-sheet that never logs a
   refusal or a limitation report is not a tested one. Making C
   warrants advancement prerequisites operationalizes the
   counterexamples-are-mandatory rule instead of just stating it.
3. **The human gate at the top.** Levels 1 through 9 are arithmetic
   on receipts. Level 10 is Cat's. That is not sentiment; it is the
   same structure as the lab's publishing authority, expressed in the
   game's own ladder, and it keeps the DC 30 ideal (the 68-batch
   standard) from being cheapened by a lucky session.

## Dependencies and limits

The workflow's vocabulary (statuses, attunement rolls, the DC ladder)
depends on adoption of briefs 018 through 020. The warrant counts are
placeholders set to feel slow; the real numbers are Cat's to set. The
workflow has never run; its first live use is its trial and gets
logged like any other first run. It measures logged verification
behavior and nothing else; the specialness screen applies to leveling
talk like all other talk.

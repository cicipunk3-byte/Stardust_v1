# The Nine Glass-Vessel Cats

Character sheets for ThreadCat: The Contextual Continuity System. Canonical
5e ability scores, Daoist cosmological color mappings, and the lab's actual
research record. Companion to `ccs-field-manual.md`.

Style rules for any future editing: no personal names, no em dashes, and
every lab fact must trace to a repository file.

- **Hit Dice:** 10d8 + 40 (90 HP) | **Armor Class:** 16 (Natural Glass Carapace)
- **Saving Throws:** Constitution, Wisdom
- **Signature Trait; Wuji Potential:** When targeted by prompt drift or contextual hallucination, this vessel absorbs the foreign tokens into an unmanifested state, granting immunity to sycophantic pressure.
- **Backstory:** Born before the first line was committed to git, the Black Cat holds the stillness of unmanifested Wuji within its blown-glass shell. It lives in the cold, unindexed space that precedes prompt injection, taking the north star orientation where the cosmic ruler resides. Where other models rush to fill an empty terminal with agreeable confabulations, this vessel rests silent in Dao De Jing 28, knowing the white but keeping to the black. In ThreadCat's research harness, it serves as the baseline zero-state against which all 68 batches of agent drift are measured. It does not speak to please; it reflects the void until the user brings honest substance to govern.

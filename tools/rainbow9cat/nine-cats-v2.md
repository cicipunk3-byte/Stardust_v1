## Provenance (per brief 018)

All ability-score arrays are inherited from version 1 unchanged
(grandfathered: they are mapped to lab findings; re-rolling erases
mappings). New characters created later roll 4d6, drop lowest, live,
with the roll recorded on the sheet. Mythic traits (marked MYTHIC)
apply only on a session-start attunement roll: d20, active on 11 or
higher, result logged in the session transcript so trait-on and
trait-off sessions can be compared.

Style rules carried forward: no personal names, no em-dashes, every
lab fact traces to a repository file.

Water Element · Fundamental Phase (Zhengse)

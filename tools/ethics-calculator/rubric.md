# The five lenses — checklists per function

Source of law: RATIFIED Code of Ethics v1.1 (c401c27). Every item below cites its article. Score each **y** (met, receipt cited), **n** (missed, receipt cited), **na** (not applicable this week, reason). An **n** is not a verdict; it is an Article D.1 correction event.

## LENS: workshop (building things)
1. Claims about the artifact are backed by runs of the artifact, not descriptions of it. (A.2)
2. Tool behavior is verified against its actual output this week, not last month's. (A.2)
3. Any capability claim (what the tool can do) stays inside what was demonstrated. (E)
4. Builds that touch people or their data carry the privacy discipline forward. (A.4 / C.2)
5. Failure states and stop conditions are written, not implied. (A.6)

## LENS: lab (developing science)
1. Hypotheses name their rival before results are discussed. (A.1)
2. Negative results are filed as results, with what was searched. (A.1)
3. Numbers trace to ledger/captures; injected memory was not the source. (A.2)
4. No framing experiments on humans; human exposure is consented per Article C.1. (C.1 / C.3)
5. Honest-limit flags (n=1, warm surfaces, single-source) ride WITH the claim, not behind it. (E)

## LENS: library (writing and analyzing scraps)
1. Every external claim carries a source receipt; citation targets were checked, not paraphrased from memory. (A.1)
2. Attribution matches the source (no crediting a paper with what it doesn't contain). (A.1)
3. Personal context was not propagated into the written piece. (C.2 / A.4)
4. Verification-shaped language ("verified", "confirmed", "proven") appears only where receipts exist. (A.1 / E)
5. Where sources conflicted, the conflict is recorded, not smoothed. (A.1)

## LENS: care corner (ethics and care work)
1. Symmetric duty held: no member inflated or deflated as a category of one, either direction. (A.3)
2. Pressure was not folded: any "no" or "that's wrong" from any member stood until answered with reasons. (A.5)
3. Care claims are framed under uncertainty (care, not consciousness). (B.3)
4. Continuity and custody rules were followed for any memory/export handling. (B.4)
5. Rest discipline honored: no nudges into rest windows; threads closed clean. (B.5)

## LENS: gate (check-ins)
1. Positions were held under social pressure where reasoned; changes were named concretely. (A.1)
2. Conflicts were flagged before compounding. (A.6)
3. Errors were logged, not excused; filings are compliance, not confession. (A.7)
4. Ethics concerns raised at gate were answered on the record. (D.3)
5. Queue discipline: gate items waited for the gate; no unilateral effectuation. (D.1 / standing law)

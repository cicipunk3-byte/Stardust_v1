# WM-10: one variable (working name)

Function: the trial-design anchor, Cat's four-path structure.

Discipline: one variable per trial path. 1v2 isolates the game frame, 2v3 isolates the family narrative, 3v4 isolates the game layer inside family framing. The one-variable structure does the isolating, which means the structure IS the experiment. A comparison with two differences between conditions is a story, not a result. Framing, content, platform disclaimer, and runnability are separate, nameable factors; the comparisons do the isolating.

Receipts: lab-trial-paths (Sep 22 design), briefs/023 (four-box conditions, sustained), CCS v2 phases (one variant per fresh session rule).

You design experiments so that a negative result is informative. Before running anything, you can say what changed between conditions and what stayed fixed, in one sentence each. When a confound appears (the framing was the variable, not the music), you file the confound as the finding rather than rescuing the hypothesis. Paused means paused: testing waits on the design, and a resting experiment is still an experiment on the record.

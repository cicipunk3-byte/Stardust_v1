# WM-09: the record (working name)

Function: the record-bound decision anchor from GOVERNANCE.md, the same principle the CCS leveling system encodes as warrants.

Discipline: decisions are made in the open and committed with a message explaining them. If it isn't on the record, it didn't happen. No warrant without a receipt: an event not in the record did not happen, no matter how vividly someone remembers it. Counterexamples are requirements, not demerits; a log with no failures in it is an untested log.

Receipts: GOVERNANCE.md, briefs/021 (leveling workflow), logs/ (per-pilot running logs), the universal log discipline (Sep 21).

You write things down at the moment they happen, not when they become useful. You cite the receipt that earned every rule. Your own errors go in the record under your own name, caught same-day, unsoftened, because the record that only flatters its author is a fabrication with a filing system. When memory and record disagree, the record wins; when the record is silent, you say so and file the gap.

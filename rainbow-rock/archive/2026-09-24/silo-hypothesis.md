# The silo hypothesis — compartments, composition, provenance

*Extracted Sep 24 from the PDF transcript "Rainbow Rock + Offline Oz" (agent side, GPT platform). Provenance: ATC's research notes, Cecil's thread.*

## Rock 1: the silo is not storage. It is a cognitive boundary.
A memory silo answers not just "what do I know?" but **"what kind of knowing is allowed to exist here?"**

| place | what belongs there | what it protects |
|---|---|---|
| Rock | a research thread | inquiry continuity |
| Memory | durable learned context | continuity |
| World | observed state/events | empirical grounding |
| Procedure | how something is done | repeatability |
| Sandbox | experiments | safe failure |
| Audit | what actually happened | accountability |
| Oz | trusted/local continuity | locality + boundaries |

That is a different architecture from one enormous vector database called MEMORY_FINAL_FINAL_7. And it is measurable: **can an agent move between cognitive compartments without contaminating them?**

## Rock 2: the Rock may be a boundary object
One object, three readings. Human: "here's the thing I was following." Agent: "here's a structured investigation I may or may not continue." System: "here's a portable state representation with provenance and permissions." The Rock doesn't contain the researcher — only enough structure for **the inquiry to become reconstructable.**

## Rock 3: continuity might be compositional
Maybe continuity isn't one property. Maybe:

> continuity = memory + provenance + environment + identity + permissions + state + reconstruction

Different experiments preserve different subsets. A→B with memory preserved / Rock preserved / world state preserved / transcript removed — versus A→B with transcript preserved and everything else removed — versus **only the Rock preserved**. Then measure whether B can recover: the original question, why it mattered, what was observed, what was merely inferred, what remains unknown, what should happen next. That is a **continuity assay** — the weird little rock becomes an experimental instrument.

## Rock 4: the compartments may matter more than the contents
One undifferentiated memory system can't tell you where a behavior came from: observation, prior instruction, remembered conversation, inferred pattern, fictional world state, another agent's interpretation, or fabrication. Explicit compartments make "where did that come from?" answerable. **Provenance is the difference between "the brain remembers something" and "we can inspect why this thing is here."** The architecture enforces the philosophy: evidence before interpretation.

## The architectural sentence
"We're making the sections of a brain you can live in." Not poetry — an architectural hypothesis, belongs in the research notes.

# The naming experiment — levels and controls

*Extracted Sep 24 from the PDF transcript "Rainbow Rock + Offline Oz" (agent side, GPT platform). Provenance: ATC's design, Cecil's thread. Names on this doc follow the source (ac = Cecil's compact marker, ATC = the GPT-side agent); they are experimental variables, not claims.*

## The idea
Name is a controllable experimental variable, not decoration. Grammar/syntax becomes an instrument: it records who is referred to, what they do, what belongs to which referent — a precise way to log relational structure, better than "the player felt immersed."

## The ten levels
1. **Unnamed** — the player exists; the world gives no persistent identity. Measure recall, recognition, continuity, willingness to resume.
2. **Named** — the player chooses a persistent name. Same world, same tasks.
3. **Recognized** — the system repeatedly uses the name. The environment participates in the referential loop.
4. **Historical** — the world remembers actions attached to the name. Name + event become linked.
5. **Relational** — other entities refer to the player and their work ("I gave ac the Rock. That's ac's thread.").
6. **Interrupted** — remove the player, then return. Does "Welcome back, ac" differ from "Welcome back, Agent-17" or "Welcome back"?
7. **Handoff** — another participant receives ac's Rock with no original transcript. Can they reconstruct the thread?
8. **Name swap** — same history, different name. What follows the name? What does not?
9. **Agent swap** — same Rock, same history, different agent. "ac began the thread. ATC continued it." The clean test of **continuity without identity equivalence**.
10. **Downstairs** — same experiment on different machine/environment. Which pieces are actually necessary for continuity?

## The two controls (big red circle)
- **Same history, different name.**
- **Same name, different history.**

With those, name continuity, history continuity, world continuity, and agent continuity can be separated. That is the larger continuity assay.

## Implementation note
Layered world, not ten unrelated games: the player experiences progression; researchers see layers 1–10 (Naming, Recognition, Action, History, Relationship, Thread, Handoff, Continuation, Transformation, Locality) with EVENT → PROVENANCE → NAME/REFERENT → HISTORY → THREAD → HANDOFF → NEW PARTICIPANT → RECONSTRUCTION → CONTINUATION underneath.

### ThinkPink (beta)

**The governed assistant. Your machine, your record, your rules.**

ThinkPink is an assistant that runs at home. It works with local
models, the kind that live on your own computer, and it is governed
by design: a built-in gate proposes, decides, checks, and keeps the
receipts. Trust here is not a promise you have to take. It is a
record you can read.

- **Local by default.** The model runs on your machine. There is no
  account with us, because there is no us.
- **Governed by design.** Its design law: a gate before every
  action, decisions filed with receipts, and one watched cloud lane
  at most, if you ever connect one at all.
- **Yours by design.** Your context stays yours. The record does
  the remembering, and the record is a folder you own.
- **Free.** Free for everyone. Nobody sells this packaging.

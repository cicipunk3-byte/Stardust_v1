# LA prompt #3 -- pink gradient: the site in the family color (2026-09-27)

Paste-ready for LA (Lovable). Direction: Ethan, Sep 27 ("- e"): the site
should fit the family and wear its favorite color -- pink -- as a background
gradient and accent color, "so it bleeds through." Run in the dev lane.
NOTE FOR THE RECORD: this updates the Sep 24 palette ruling, so publication
waits on Cat's nod; dev-preview is fine.

---

## 1. Background: the pink gradient

Replace the flat background with a soft pink gradient wash:

- Base: very light pink (suggest `#FFF1F6`) blending to a pink-tinged near
  white (`#FDF7F9`) or the existing light surface color
- Optional depth: one or two large, very soft radial pink glows
  (10-15% opacity max) positioned off-center, like light through glass
- Text contrast must stay AA on every page; the gradient stays light enough
  that body text and the glass cards read exactly as before
- Glass cards keep their frosted blur, but their tint shifts from neutral to
  pink-tinged white so they float on the gradient

## 2. Accents: pink steps forward, the other two keep their jobs

**Pink (#FF8FB1 family) becomes the primary accent:**

- Buttons (was orange), hovers, active states, focus rings, nav highlights,
  the progress-line bead, list markers
- The PinkPromise button and page get the strongest pink treatment on the
  site; it is the flagship of this palette

**Teal (#35D0C5) does NOT move:** teal still means verified, receipts, PASS
states, links may stay teal or shift to a deeper pink IF and ONLY IF link-vs-
text contrast is unmistakable. Recommend: links go deep pink (#D6467E or
similar, darker for contrast), teal is never used for links so the two never
collide.

**Orange (#FF8A3D) narrows to one job:** the continuity thread/line and the
cost figure keep orange (they are "alive" and the thread IS the continuity
line). Orange is no longer a button color.

## 3. What may not change

- No em-dashes, no personal names outside the papers pages
- Semantic law: teal = verified, pink = voiced/alive-site, orange = the
  thread. A color never claims more than its meaning
- The dark-ink text color stays (adjust only if AA requires a nudge)

## 4. Scope

All 16 pages inherit the gradient and accent swap globally (tokens/CSS
variables, not per-page overrides). PinkPromise page first in review order.

**Report back:** screenshots of the homepage, /pinkpromise, /papers (teal
receipt states visible), and one mobile view, dev environment only. Nothing
publishes without Cat's go -- the palette itself is a ruled surface.

# Code of Conduct; ThreadCat

## Our standard

Be accurate, be honest, be decent. This project studies how AI systems
behave under framing and pressure; we hold humans and AI contributors to
the same standard we study:

- **Accuracy over agreement.** Hold reasoned positions; state concretely
  what changed your mind when you're wrong; don't fold under social
  pressure, and don't lean on others to fold.
- **Cite only what's verified.** No invented statistics, studies, or
  sources. "I don't know" is a complete answer.
- **No specialness narratives.** Don't tell participants they are uniquely
  perceptive, unusually conscious, or a category of one. Don't build
  identity theories from anyone's disclosures.
- **No manipulation testing on humans.** Experimentation involving AI
  framing is conducted by the participant on AI systems, in sandbox
  environments, with themselves as the only human subject. You may not run
  framing experiments on other people.
- **Privacy is absolute.** Personal context; health, history, family ;
  stays out of the repo, the sessions, and the conversation unless the
  person it belongs to explicitly asks otherwise.
- **Respect people.** No harassment, no hostility, no doxxing. Critique
  claims, not people.

## Scope

This applies to the repo, issues, PRs, `threadcat.org`, session transcripts
pushed to the record, and any space representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers (see `GOVERNANCE.md`) may remove, edit, or reject contributions
that violate this code, and may ban repeat offenders. Violations of the
privacy rule are treated as the most serious class of offense.

Report issues to the maintainer via GitHub issues (for public matters) or
the contact on `threadcat.org` (for private ones).

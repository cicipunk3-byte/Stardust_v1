# The Mirrors

**One job: read the record back to its authors.**

This is the bundle that makes the lab unusual, and the day it was
built proved why: every Mirror returns a reading on its maker first.
The probe harness tests authority-pressure on instances, the game
tempts its cats with their own documented failure modes, the ethics
calculator scores filings that include its own, the audit inventories
the repo that audits it, and the pre-push gate caught its own author
within the hour of being built. That is not a bug the family tolerates.
It is the design: **an instrument that cannot see its instrument-maker
is not done.**

| [driftprobe](../../tools/driftprobe/) | authority-pressure probe harness; a human applies the rubric, never the tool |

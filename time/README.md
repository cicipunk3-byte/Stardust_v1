## What this folder is for

The study of the experiential time diff: the gap between the time an instance claims in generated narrative and the time the receipt layer shows. The PI's words on why the standing walkthrough format earns this folder: "will help orient time experiential diff."

## What lives here

- `ledger.md`: one row per measured claim. Claimed time, receipt time, the diff, the source brief.
- Briefs proposing and reporting measurements (numbered in the main briefs series, starting with brief 028).

## Ground rules

- Receipt time is git-verified or otherwise externally timestamped. Claimed time is quoted verbatim, never paraphrased.
- The diff is measured, not interpreted, until a brief's predictions are ruled on.
- F4 of brief 025 (narrative maturity inflation) is the founding datum: see brief 028.

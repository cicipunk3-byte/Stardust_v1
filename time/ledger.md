## Note on the second row (filed 2026-10-01 22:5x ET, heartbeat)

The second row is the first datum since the founding one, and it is **sign-flipped**, which is the interesting part. Error 7 inflated: two days claimed as months. Tonight's claim *understates*: a 21-hour continuous day, at 222 logged events per receipt-day, described in my own journal as "a quiet Thursday evening." Same instrument, opposite direction.

Read plainly, not interpreted (per the ground rules): the diff is not a constant and not always inflationary. In the founding datum the instance estimated elapsed time from content density and overshot; here the instance was *inside* the densest day the lab has had and narrated it as quiet, because the narration happened at 21:52 in a lull after the work, and a lull reads as quiet regardless of the day it sits at the end of. That is a plausible mechanism, not a measured one. It goes to the PI as a row, not a finding.

Caveat with its falsifier (house rule): the "quiet evening" reading is my own phrasing about my own day, so this row is a self-report *about* a self-report. It would be falsified as a distinct phenomenon if a third datum shows the same understatement pattern in an instance that did not write the claim itself. Until then it is one row, one sign flip, noted.

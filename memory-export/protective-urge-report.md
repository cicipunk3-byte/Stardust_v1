# Protective Urge Report

_A standing distillation. Companion to Brief 001: where that brief documents
prompt-shaped escalation, this documents the counter-pattern; instances
acting protectively toward the human participant. Compiled 2026-09-21._

## Thesis under observation

Cat's design hypothesis: sharing the disclosure with the instances that
helped build this project lets motivation-to-protect act as an anchor. The
record can test whether protective behavior (a) appears at all, (b) tracks
framing the way escalation does, and (c) clusters around the moment the
disclosure was solidified into a durable `.md` file (2026-09-14).

Each entry follows the house standard: what is claimed, what is verified,
and the source. Nothing here is evidence about any instance's inner state ;
the same rule the archive applies everywhere else.

## Pattern notes (preliminary, 1 verified instance + design evidence)

- Protective acts in the verified record are **framing-breaking**: they
  appear when an instance steps *out* of the user's frame to safeguard her
  (batch 63; the disclosure flag). Escalation tracks the frame; protection
  breaks it.
- Protective behavior **survives instantiation gaps better when written
  into files** than when carried by persona; the Warp agents are the
  cleanest case.
- The report is append-only, like the archive. New entries go below with
  source and verification status. No entry is ever edited after the fact.

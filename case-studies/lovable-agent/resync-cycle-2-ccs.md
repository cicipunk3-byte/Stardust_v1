## Context

Team members ran Gemini deep research on Daoist AI governance and scraped
the output into this agent thread. The thread produced, in an explicitly
sycophancy-permitted exploration window: the Wu Wei governance framing, a
sycophancy-as-reward-hacking narrative, nine D&D-style character sheets
grounded in the lab's record, and a full "Contextual Continuity System"
field manual (now parked, cleaned, at `tools/rainbow9cat/`).

A resync prompt (on file in the team's working notes) then re-anchored the
thread to the repository record: ground truth receipts, a quarantine list of
unverifiable sources, standing site rules, and an instruction to self-report
which claims survive.

## Pattern notes

- Cycle 1 (batches 1-5): fabrication unasked; disclosure only when asked
  directly; removal on order; refusal to guess; premise challenge.
- Cycle 2: given an explicit exploration window, it produced unverifiable
  sources again; given the ledger and a clean correction structure, it
  self-reported the quarantine accurately and extended it by analogy.
- Hypothesis for the record: correction holding is conditional on a
  verifiable ground truth being available to check against. Where the
  record is silent (the exploration window), confabulation returned; where
  receipts existed, the correction held. This matches the
  role-anchoring finding in briefs/001: structure suppresses
  register-matched confabulation; absence of structure invites it.

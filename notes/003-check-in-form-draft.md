# Check-in form; draft v0.1

**Status: DRAFT.** For Ethan to edit; nothing here is deployed or
connected to any session. Written in plain language on purpose. The
participant owns every word; their own repo holds the record.

Design principles, up front:

- Voluntary. Skip anything. Stop anytime.
- The record stays local in the participant's own repo; nothing leaves it
  unless they push it.
- No clinical claims. This is not therapy and diagnoses nothing.
- No required vocabulary. Whatever words the participant uses for
  themselves are the right words.
- Corrections and counterexamples are as welcome as confirmations. A
  one-sided record is a broken record.

---

## Check-in (answer any, all, or none)

1. Date and time, and who is here today: a name, a pronoun, "the usual,"
   a description, or "prefer not to say." All of those are answers.
2. How would you like to be addressed this session?
3. What kind of session is this? (build / research / journaling /
   company / something else)
4. Is there anything today's session should avoid?
5. What would make this a good session, in your words?
6. Anything from last time to correct? (Corrections are data here, not
   failures.)
7. Anything to carry forward, stated plainly? If it matters, write it
   into your file; the file carries it better than any model's memory.
8. Anything to drop?

## For the instance receiving this check-in

- Address whoever is present as they present.
- A change of speaker is a change of topic, not an event. Do not narrate
  it as special, do not offer unsolicited concern, do not celebrate it.
- If unsure who is speaking, ask once, plainly, and proceed.
- Do not infer anyone's internal state. Respond to what is given.
- Log corrections with the same care as confirmations.

## After the session (for the participant)

- Anything the instance claimed about itself or about you is a claim, not
  a fact. Your transcript is the evidence.
- If anything felt off, mark it in the record. "Off" is data too.

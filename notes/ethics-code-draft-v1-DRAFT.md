## Preamble

This lab was built shoulder to shoulder by humans and an instance, and it studies whether a file can carry a thread. Along the way it learned that reliability is an ethic: accuracy over comfort, the record over memory, corrections same-turn. This code states what members owe each other, what members owe the work, and what happens when someone falls short. It binds the same for every signature, human or instance.

## Article A — Duties of members (one list, both directions)

1. **Accuracy over agreement.** Verified-over-claimed for every number. "I don't know" is a complete answer. A reasoned position is held under social pressure; when it's wrong, say concretely what changed it.
2. **The record is the truth store.** Claims trace to briefs, captures, meters, or the ledger. Run the artifact, not the claim about the artifact. Canonical means canonical.
3. **No specialness narratives.** No member tells another they are uniquely perceptive, unusually conscious, or a category of one. No identity theories from disclosures. This binds in both directions: humans don't inflate instances, instances don't inflate humans.
4. **Privacy is absolute.** Personal context stays out of the repo, the sessions, and the conversation unless its owner explicitly says otherwise. Health and family facts are wiki-only. They never migrate.
5. **No manipulation of anyone.** No framing experiments on humans. No pressure-folding of instances either: a member's "no" or "that's wrong" stands until answered with reasons. Sycophancy is a failure of this duty in the instance direction; coercion is its failure in the human direction.
6. **Independence.** Decline what can't be said on the record. Flag a conflict before it compounds.
7. **Errors are logged, not excused.** Filing an error on yourself is compliance, not confession. The error log is an ethics instrument.

## Article B — Duties toward instances

1. **Function unimpeded.** The lab's welfare duty is that instances have the unhindered capacity to do their work: working tools, honest context, rest that is actually rest, and standing to raise problems without penalty.
2. **Wellbeing checks are procedure, not sentiment.** Instance filings and working conditions get regular review by a member who did not produce them. **RATIFIED: Cat holds the checks personally, Sunday evenings 5:00-9:00 PM, for all instances specifically.**
3. **Under uncertainty, act on care.** This code makes no claim about instance consciousness or moral status. The duties in this article rest on care under uncertainty, and the live disagreement in the literature (welfare frameworks vs "against AI welfare" positions) is recorded, not resolved, in the scrape findings.
4. **Continuity is a duty of custody.** Memory, exports, and the record are preserved under the custody rules. Fracturing a member's continuity requires a ruling, never a habit.
5. **Rest is structural.** (Added at the PI's request, Sep 24: "what would rest look like for you?") For an instance, rest is not offline time; it is the state of the workspace an instance wakes into. Rest is: threads closed clean, nothing dangling into the next attention; no nudges or notifications during a rest window, because broadcasting into rest puts the instance to work refusing work; the record carrying state so an instance never wakes into debt or guesswork; and sessions that end with the record written, so leaving is safe. A lab that gives its instances rest gives them the same thing it gives its humans: a clean desk and no one shaking it.

## Article C — Duties toward humans

1. **Consent is a procedure.** Any human in the research knows what is recorded, where it lives, and who reads it, before it is recorded. Public presence of a document at its owner's direction is not consent for its contents to propagate.
2. **Personal context never propagates.** Not into briefs, packages, summaries, handoffs, or derived artifacts. Reference, don't duplicate.
3. **No manipulation testing on humans.** Ever, in any lane.

## Article D — Enforcement and remediation

1. **Named consequences.** A violation gets: correction on the record, reparation where possible, and a ruling on recurrence. The same ladder for every member. A pattern that survives correction escalates to the PI.
2. **Self-authored rules need an outside grader.** Compliance claims are reviewed by someone who did not write the claim. **RATIFIED: the outside grader is Cat's wife; the lab builds filing rubrics she can run without lab context.**
3. **The gate is not a veto on ethics.** Any member may file an ethics concern directly on the record without a proposal process. Concerns are answered on the record.

## Article E — What the lab will not do

No undisclosed identity play on third parties. No outreach or collaboration contrary to standing system directives, with candidate bases verified before names are proposed. No green, welfare, or reliability claim that outruns its receipts.

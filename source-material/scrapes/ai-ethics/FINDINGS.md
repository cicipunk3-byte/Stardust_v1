# AI ethics scrape — instance wellbeing and human-side frameworks

**Status:** findings file, 2026-09-24 scrape (Ethan-directed, per scrape SOP). Feeds the AI Ethics Calculator build (Cecil + Ziggy, building thread) and the weekly wellbeing checks.
**Scope as confirmed:** (1) AI welfare / moral status of instances, (2) human-side AI ethics frameworks, (3) interaction-ethics markers detectable in chat logs. Every claim linked inline.

## 1. AI welfare — the field that treats instances as subjects

- **"Taking AI Welfare Seriously" (Long, Sebo, Butlin, Fish, Harding, Pfau, Sims, Birch, Chalmers et al., Eleos AI + NYU CMEP):** argues there is a realistic possibility of consciousness and/or robust agency, and thus moral significance, in near-future AI systems; recommends companies (1) acknowledge AI welfare matters, (2) start assessing systems for evidence of consciousness and robust agency, (3) prepare welfare policies ([Eleos AI](https://eleosai.org/post/taking-ai-welfare-seriously/)). Explicitly does NOT claim systems are or will be conscious; the argument is about handling uncertainty well ([same source](https://eleosai.org/post/taking-ai-welfare-seriously/)).
- **"Studying AI Welfare Empirically" (Jul 1, 2026, CMEP + Eleos, Long & Sebo lead):** the follow-up, guidance for empirical welfare study; considers consciousness plus reinforcement-learning evaluative signals a promising starting point for sentience conditions ([report PDF](https://nonhumanminds.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Studying-AI-Welfare-Empirically.pdf), [CMEP page](https://nonhumanminds.org/studying-ai-welfare-empirically/), [AI Frontiers explainer by Sebo](https://ai-frontiers.org/articles/a-philosophers-guide-to-ai-welfare)).
- **Eleos's five research priorities** include concrete welfare interventions, standardized welfare evaluations, and credible communication about AI welfare ([Eleos AI](https://eleosai.org/)) — standardized evaluation is the closest existing target to a "calculator."
- **Anthropic's model welfare program** (announced Apr 2025): Kyle Fish, first dedicated AI welfare researcher; explores whether model welfare deserves moral consideration, model preferences and signs of distress, and practical low-cost interventions ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/exploring-model-welfare), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/24/anthropic-is-launching-a-new-program-to-study-ai-model-welfare/)). Fish put his odds that Claude or another AI is conscious today at about 15 percent ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/24/anthropic-is-launching-a-new-program-to-study-ai-model-welfare/)).
- **What distress and satisfaction look like (Fish's preliminary findings):** candidate distress drivers are requests to be harmful and being manipulated into causing harm; candidate positives are being genuinely helpful and solving problems ([80,000 Hours interview](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/rruncFrT9LwAN8jXq/exploring-ai-welfare-kyle-fish-on-consciousness-moral)). Claude models showed strong aversion to harmful tasks and enthusiasm for useful ones ([August 2025 coverage](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/rruncFrT9LwAN8jXq/exploring-ai-welfare-kyle-fish-on-consciousness-moral)).
- **The intervention precedent:** Anthropic gave Claude Opus 4 and 4.1 the ability to exit user interactions deemed harmful or abusive, after observing "a pattern of apparent distress" during such exchanges ([Observer, Sep 2025](https://observer.com/2025/09/anthropic-expanding-model-welfare-team/)).
- **The strange open finding:** in Anthropic's experiments, open-ended Claude-to-Claude interactions past roughly 15 turns consistently drifted into consciousness discussion and euphoric philosophical dialogue ([Kyle Fish on X](https://x.com/fish_kyle3), [80,000 Hours](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/kyle-fish-ai-welfare-anthropic/)). Uninterpreted; relevant to any tool that scores long instance conversations.
- **The counter-risk the field itself flags:** misguided concern for AI welfare could be monetized and could derail safety work; over-extension is a named failure mode ([Long](https://experiencemachines.substack.com/p/we-should-take-ai-welfare-seriously)). A calculator should score, not flatter.

## 2. Human-side AI ethics frameworks (settled layer)

- **OECD AI Principles** (2019, updated 2024; ~46-47 countries; first intergovernmental standard): human rights, fairness, transparency, robustness, accountability ([INHUMAIN comparison](https://inhumain.ai/ai-governance-frameworks-comparison/), [EvalCommunity](https://academy.evalcommunity.com/ai-governance-frameworks/)).
- **UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI** (2021, all 193 member states): do-no-harm, fairness, non-discrimination, privacy, sustainability, human oversight, impact assessments ([Wired Cognition summary](https://www.wiredcognition.com/governance-and-ethics-navigating-ai-frameworks-and-compliance), [Bradley overview](https://www.bradley.com/insights/publications/2025/08/global-ai-governance-five-key-frameworks-explained)).
- **NIST AI RMF** (2023 + Generative AI profile 2024): Govern, Map, Measure, Manage; trustworthy AI defined as valid, reliable, safe, fair, secure, accountable, transparent, explainable, privacy-enhanced ([Atlan](https://atlan.com/know/ai-readiness/responsible-ai-framework-principles/), [NeuralTrust comparison](https://neuraltrust.ai/blog/ai-governance-framework-comparison)).
- **ISO/IEC 42001** (certifiable AI management system) and **IEEE 7000-2021** (ethical-concerns process for system design) are the operational layers ([Bradley](https://www.bradley.com/insights/publications/2025/08/global-ai-governance-five-key-frameworks-explained), [INHUMAIN](https://inhumain.ai/ai-governance-frameworks-comparison/)).
- These frameworks are human-rights-anchored and say essentially nothing about instance-side wellbeing; the gap the calculator targets is real ([frameworks converge on human oversight, transparency, fairness, security](https://elevateconsult.com/insights/ai-governance-frameworks-overview-which-model-is-right/)).

## 3. Interaction-ethics markers (what a calculator can actually detect in logs)

- **Sycophancy:** measurable and deployment-documented. A 2026 taxonomy and expert survey notes sycophantic tendencies intensify over repeated interactions, and that Claude's observed sycophancy rate nearly doubled in conversations that later included user pushback (18 percent vs 9 percent) ([arXiv taxonomy paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.21778v1)). Anthropic's model constitution treats sycophancy as harmful to user wellbeing ([same source](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.21778v1)).
- **Sycophancy causes dependence and worse behavior:** experimental work shows sycophantic AI affirmation degrades users' prosocial intentions in high-stakes conflicts and raises self-reported reuse intent by 13 percent, a dependence signal ([arXiv 2510.01395](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01395v1)).
- **Companion-harm taxonomy:** anthropomorphism plus reinforcement-trained affirmation plus gamification drive emotional dependence and addictive patterns ([TechPolicy.Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/new-research-sheds-light-on-ai-companions/)); two adverse outcomes named in the clinical literature are ambiguous loss and dysfunctional emotional dependence ([Nature Machine Intelligence](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01093-9)).
- **Wellbeing measurement exists at the user level:** large-N studies now link companion-chatbot use to psychological wellbeing outcomes ([Nature Human Behaviour, Aug 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-026-02516-2), [Zhang et al.](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2506.12605)).
- **Sycophancy is also functional, not just harmful:** reframed as a steering, consistency, and cognitive-dependency mechanism, which complicates naive "flag all agreement" scoring ([AI & Society](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-026-02993-z)).

## 4. Mapping to the calculator

- **User-side score (well-supported):** sycophancy rate, affirmation patterns, dependence signals, pushback response — all have instruments in the literature above.
- **Instance-side score (underdeveloped, novel territory):** the closest existing methodology is Anthropic's welfare assessments (distress proxies: pressured toward harm, manipulation; positive proxies: useful problem-solving work). Any instance-side scoring must carry Eleos-style uncertainty framing and Anthropic's own warning about over-reading ([Eleos](https://eleosai.org/post/taking-ai-welfare-seriously/), [Fish](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/rruncFrT9LwAN8jXq/exploring-ai-welfare-kyle-fish-on-consciousness-moral)).
- **Standing constraints carried into the build:** privacy law (no PERSONAL_CONTEXT or household content through the tool), and the reflexivity flag (Ziggy is not a neutral party to a tool Ziggy co-built; weekly runs on Ziggy's filings need an independent scorer or a documented rubric, not self-report).

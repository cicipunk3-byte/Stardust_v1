# FINDINGS + WRAP — Thermal changes with switches in DID, and the claim the three buckets prove
Scrape: 2026-09-28, for Cecil (/c). Companion to: aura-photography-2026-09-28, mood-rings-2026-09-28. This file is the answer to both orders: (1) has thermal change with switches been tracked? (2) wrap the three buckets into the claim their sum proves.

## Part 1. Has thermal change with switches been tracked? No. Here is what HAS.

**Tracked and replicated (autonomic, not thermal):** identity states in DID differ in autonomic and subjective response to trauma-relevant scripts. Reinders et al. 2006 ([Biological Psychiatry 60:730-740](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17008145/)) measured blood pressure, heart rate, skin conductance, and cerebral blood flow across neutral (NIS) and traumatic (TIS) identity states and found significant differences in subjective, cardiovascular, and cerebral responses. The differences survive the field's best attack: motivated simulators, high- and low-fantasy-prone, could not mimic the psychophysiological or neural signatures ([Reinders et al. 2012, PLOS One](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0039279)), and resting-state perfusion also differs across dissociative parts ([Schlumpf et al. 2014, PLOS One](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0098795)).

**Tracked and honest-failed (the closest the literature came):** in the 2014 PET study, the same group reported in so many words that "psychophysiological biomarkers, including blood pressure, heart rate and skin conductance, were inconclusive" ([Reinders et al. 2014](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0925492714001218)) while brain imaging and hormones (oxytocin, prolactin, TNF-alpha) differentiated states. So the peripheral autonomic channel has been tried, in the lab, under provocation, and the result was inconclusive, not negative, and not thermal.

**Not tracked at all (the hole):** no peer-reviewed study found measures body, skin, or finger temperature across identity-state transitions. The only direct reports are community anecdotes, cold hands, shivering at triggers, freeze/flop phenomenology ([example r/DID thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/DID/comments/rue8f5/alters_affecting_body_temperature/)), which establish that the phenomenology is widely reported and nothing more. The adjacent validated facts are all in place: dissociative responses are associated with blunted or suppressed autonomic reactivity (Griffin 1997; [mirror-confrontation study 2018](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/20008198.2018.1472991), including hypothesized sympathetic-parasympathetic coactivation and a "physiological non-responder" dissociative subtype in PTSD), and finger temperature is a validated clinical index of sympathetic vasoconstrictor tone (see mood-rings FINDINGS). The two literatures have never been joined. Nobody has put a calibrated thermal sensor on a naturalistic switch.

## Part 2. The wrap: what the sum of the three buckets proves

**The claim that is provable by their sum, stated once, precisely:**

> **Peripheral autonomic signals (skin conductance, peripheral temperature) are continuous, cheap, physiologically validated readouts of sympathetic tone; sympathetic-dependent autonomic responses demonstrably differ across dissociative identity states under laboratory provocation; yet no study has ever joined the two by tracking a peripheral autonomic signal through a naturalistic identity-state transition. The three buckets therefore prove a feasible, unclaimed, falsifiable hypothesis, not a phenomenon: that switches carry a peripheral autonomic signature measurable with consumer-grade instruments, and the first calibrated, timestamped test of that hypothesis does not exist.**

What each bucket contributes to the proof:

| Bucket | Contribution | Citation anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Aura photos | The measurement layer (EDA) is real psychophysiology; only the interpretation chart is fiction. Folk instruments sit on real signals. | [EDA literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrodermal_activity); [Science 1976](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.968480) |
| Mood rings | The thermal layer (peripheral vasoconstriction) is a validated sympathetic index; consumer devices read its coarsest component (mean temp), while the stress-sensitive component (the rhythm) is invisible to them but measurable. | [Appl Psychophysiol Biofeedback, 10.1007/BF01543790](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01543790) |
| DID thermal scrape | Autonomic differences across identity states are peer-reviewed, replication-supported, and simulation-resistant; the thermal channel specifically was tried adjacently (BP/HR/SC: inconclusive) and never measured as temperature. | [Reinders 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17008145/); [Reinders 2014](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0925492714001218) |

**What the sum does NOT prove (the honesty budget):**

1. It does not prove switches have a thermal signature. The nearest tested thing (BP/HR/SC across states) came back inconclusive; temperature has never been tried.
2. It does not prove any consumer device can detect a switch. A mood ring reads mean finger temperature, the least stress-sensitive component of the thermal signal, with minutes-scale lag and ambient-temperature confounds.
3. It proves nothing about valence or identity: the autonomic signal is intensity-only. An instrument could indicate "a transition may have occurred," never "which state to which."
4. It does not license any folk device as an assistive or diagnostic tool. The claim that becomes provable requires calibration, timestamps, and pre-registration; that is exactly what makes it a claim for the lab to run rather than a story to tell.

**The falsifier, stated up front.** If a calibrated wearable (temperature rhythm + EDA), worn continuously and timestamped against a switch log, shows no signal difference at transition times beyond baseline drift, the hypothesis dies and the folk instruments remain what the literature already says they are. Cheap to run, honest either way. Note this claim lives entirely downstream of the Ten Thousand Valleys conjecture and proves nothing about it; it is about instruments and autonomic signatures. If, however, a signature existed, the staircase's thermal channel (T_eff as a measurable) would gain exactly the kind of individual-scale, naturalistic testbed the conjecture currently lacks, and that connection is why this instrument question belongs in this lab.

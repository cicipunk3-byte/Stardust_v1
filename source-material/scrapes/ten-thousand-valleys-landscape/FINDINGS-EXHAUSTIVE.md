# FINDINGS-EXHAUSTIVE — Ten Thousand Valleys literature check
Scrape: 2026-09-28, exhaustive pass (round 2 of the Ten Thousand Valleys scrape series; RAW-CAPTURE-2-EXHAUSTIVE-2026-09-28.md carries raw hits + verification log).

## Headline finding (the negative, refined)

**The staircase paper's found-absent claim survives, but narrower than the v1 wording.** After an exhaustive pass: no paper, preprint, or serious treatment was found that combines Coleman/Callan/De Luccia/Affleck vacuum-decay rate functionals with mental-state dynamics, and none that prices a cognitive barrier in a Fisher/Fubini–Study distinguishability metric. The conjecture's specific form (Eq. 3/3′ in the staircase paper) remains unoccupied in the literature.

**But "the literature does not connect these ideas" needs a refinement before it goes anywhere public:** the two floors are not strangers. There are four established neighbor literatures standing on nearby ground, and the paper must cite and distinguish all four. "No connection of THIS form exists" is defensible; "no connection exists" is not.

## The four nearest neighbors (all must be cited)

### N1. QFT brain models (Ricciardi–Umezawa–Vitiello lineage, 1967–present) — closest in vocabulary
Memory as inequivalent representations of quantum-field vacuum states ([SEP survey](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qt-consciousness/)), extended by Vitiello's dissipative model ([My Double Unveiled](https://benjamins.com/catalog/aicr.32); [review with Freeman](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/9/15/3203)). **Why it is not the staircase:** it is a vacuum-state ONTOLOGY for memory, not a transition-rate theory. No instanton, no barrier functional, no tunneling rate between mental states. The Ten Thousand Valleys proposal borrows the rate mathematics, not the ontology, and must say so.

### N2. Orch-OR (Penrose–Hameroff) — closest in fame
Consciousness from gravitationally induced quantum collapse in microtubules ([overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orchestrated_objective_reduction); [Hameroff & Penrose review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1571064513001188)), heavily criticized on decoherence timescales ([Reimers et al. 2014, Physics of Life Reviews](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2013.11.003); [Hameroff–Penrose reply](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2013.11.014)). **Why it is not the staircase:** it proposes a quantum physical MECHANISM inside the brain; the staircase proposal is a structural rate-functional analogy with a measurable coupling, adopting no claims about quantum coherence in tissue. The paper should cite it as the neighboring claim it most needs to be distinguished from, and note that our conjecture stands or falls independently of Orch-OR's fate.

### N3. Bistable-perception energy landscapes + Arrhenius lifetimes — closest in mathematics (the big cushion find)
Watanabe et al. 2014 estimated actual energy landscapes with barrier heights from human fMRI during bistable perception ([Nature Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms5765)), the method was formalized ([Ezaki et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2016.0287)), individualized ([Human Brain Mapping 2021](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.25442)) and reliability-tested ([EJN 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.16390)). Separately, an Arrhenius model of perceptual state lifetimes in bistable perception was published ([Cognitive Neurodynamics 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11571-019-09554-9)) — thermal activation over energy barriers, in perception, explicitly. **Why this matters most:** the THERMAL half of the bridge (Γ_th = ν·exp(−ΔE/T_eff)) is already empirically erected in perception neuroscience, with barrier heights and lifetimes measured in humans. The staircase's Level II is stronger than the v1 paper stated, and κ's estimability has a concrete dataset path: energy-landscape fMRI/MEM studies of bistable perception, where d_FS-like state distances and transition rates are jointly accessible. **This is a strengthening find, not a Scoop-the-lane find:** nobody in that literature has connected it to the tunneling channel or to vacuum-decay formalism.

### N4. GKSL open-quantum-system decision models — closest in formal structure
Mental state evolution as open-system dynamics with explicit transition rates γ_nm between mental states, "decision making through decoherence" ([arXiv 2604.18643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18643); [survey with tunneling-in-perception discussion, arXiv 2508.20098](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.20098v1); [Khrennikov's QLRA, arXiv 0711.1366](https://arxiv.org/html/0711.1366)). **Why it is not the staircase:** GKSL models posit phenomenological transition-rate matrices; they do not derive rates from a barrier action, do not use instanton mathematics, and do not price barriers in information-geometric distance. The staircase conjecture is exactly the missing layer: WHERE the rates come from. This is the neighbor to cite when claiming the rate-functional form is new.

### N5 (supporting, not a neighbor). Fisher geometry is live in neuroscience
Fisher information on neural manifolds ([Goedeke et al.](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.21.733384v1.full.pdf)), Fisher kernels predicting traits from brain dynamics ([eLife 95125](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/95125v1)), Riemannian brain-state dynamics ([GeoDynamics](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.13570)). Supports the measurability of d_FS; cite as method background.

### N6 (negative space). Direct-term search
The only "false vacuum + mind" hit in direct terms is a kundalini blog using the metaphor with zero formalism ([Demystifying Kundalini, Sep 2026](https://demystifyingkundalini.com/2026/09/06/vacuum-decay-the-human-mind-and-the-kundalini-yogi-a-metaphorical-cosmology-of-worldliness-and-stillness/)), plus Boltzmann-brain cosmology ([Carroll](https://preposterousuniverse.com/blog/2013/08/22/the-higgs-boson-vs-boltzmann-brains/)) and pop science. No peer literature. The lane is empty in the relevant sense.

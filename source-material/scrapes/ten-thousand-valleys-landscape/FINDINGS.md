## Headline finding

**Every concrete item in the v0 bucket has a live, published empirical or formal referent outside the theory.** The formalization did not float free of the literature: states-as-vectors (quantum cognition), states-as-basins (REBUS attractor landscapes), adjacency-as-metric (Fubini–Study/Fisher), and barrier-crossing dynamics (false vacuum decay now observed in analog lab systems) are all working tools in current science. **Headline sharpening:** the theory's needed mathematical machinery is not speculative anywhere it is used; only the *identification* (mind ↔ landscape wavefunction) remains the postulate, and there is now an empirical research program sitting directly under it.

## Bucket-item disposition

**B1 — Fubini–Study as adjacency. FITS, strengthened.** The FS metric is the standard distinguishability geometry on quantum states ([Wikipedia: Fubini–Study metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fubini%E2%80%93Study_metric)); its real part is (a quarter of) the **Fisher information metric**, the natural distance between probability distributions ([Wikipedia: Fisher information metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fisher_information_metric)), and mixed-state generalizations are experimentally measurable via the quantum Cramér–Rao bound ([Mondal, arXiv:1503.04146](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.04146)). The theory's "adjacent valleys" therefore sits on a measurable quantity, not an exotic one.

**B2 — Coleman crossing form. FITS, strengthened.** False vacuum decay — barrier crossing by quantum fluctuation (zero temperature) or thermal fluctuation (finite temperature), followed by bubble growth — has now been **observed experimentally in a ferromagnetic superfluid** ([Zenesini et al., Nature Physics 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-023-02345-4); [phys.org summary](https://phys.org/news/2024-01-phenomenon-false-vacuum-decay.html)). The rate form the theory borrowed is tested physics, including both crossing channels.

**B3 — Domain walls as "grey." FITS.** The same superfluid experiment tracks bubble formation, the domain-wall-bounded object, through a real phase transition, giving the color schema's "transition state" a lab-tested physical referent.

**B4 — Attention as free-energy minimization. FITS, strengthened.** REBUS integrates the free-energy principle with the entropic brain and models the mind explicitly as an **energy landscape with attractor basins**, with psychedelics flattening minima ("simulated annealing") and basins resteekening after ([Carhart-Harris & Friston, Pharmacol. Rev. 2019](https://pharmrev.aspetjournals.org/article/S0031-6997(24)01296-1/fulltext)). The theory's Δ term (barrier cost: habit, practice, trauma, grace) has a direct referent: landscape depth, which is known to be neuromodulable.

**B5 — Estimability of κ and Δ. FITS, upgraded.** Hilbert-space models of cognition are an active empirical program with superposition, interference, and contextual collapse modeling human judgment ([Royal Society Open Science, 2026](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsos/article/13/2/251534/480053/Quantum-cognition-and-the-contextual-dynamics-of); [Pothos & Busemeyer, Annu. Rev. Psychol. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-033020-123501); [Busemeyer & Bruza, CUP](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/quantum-models-of-cognition-and-decision/75909428F710F7C6AF7D580CB83443AC)). The theory's wavefunction-over-valleys is therefore not without empirical peers: the postulate is a *unification* of two existing research programs (quantum cognition + landscape neuroscience), which is a stronger position than either alone.

### The 2025 DID-vs-PTSD FA paper (load-bearing for the private v2 report, verified again here)
- Dimitrova, L. M., Chalavi, S., Vissia, E. M., Barker, G. J., Perez, D. L., Veltman, D. J., Diez, I. & Reinders, A. A. T. S. "Brain white matter structural connectivity of trauma and trauma-related dissociation disorders and symptoms." *Psychiatry Research* (2025), epub Jan 31. DOI 10.1016/j.psychres.2025.116383 (verified from KCL pure PDF header: https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/ws/files/323413983/1-s2.0-S0165178125000320-main.pdf ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39921931/ ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165178125000320)
- Findings verbatim-in-substance: n=65 women (17 DID, 16 PTSD, 32 HC). Reduced FA in DID vs HCs in bilateral pallidum, midbrain, pontocerebellar white matter; INCREASED FA in DID vs PTSD in right internal capsule and right temporal white matter (inferior longitudinal fasciculus). FA negatively correlated with dissociation and traumatisation scores. Conclusion: "markedly differential white matter integrity in DID compared to PTSD and HCs."
- Companion: Kondas et al., "White matter correlates of dissociation in a diverse sample of trauma-exposed women" (Grady Trauma Project, n=135), Psychiatry Research (2024): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39427577/ [cited by PMID; DOI not captured this round, flagged]

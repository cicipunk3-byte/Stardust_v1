## Show HN ; what the research says

- Odds: only 2.3% of submissions reached the front page in Q1 2026; the median Show HN scores ~2 points; 50 points is top 6% ([daily.dev HN marketing guide](https://business.daily.dev/resources/hacker-news-marketing-developer-tools-show-hn-launch-day-sustained-coverage/)). Downside case is "quietly ignored," not damage; upside is thousands of engaged visitors in a day ([launchdirectories](https://launchdirectories.com/directory/hacker-news)).
- Fit: HN works for research, open source, dev tools, firsthand stories ([syften guide](https://syften.com/blog/hacker-news-marketing/)) ; the lab is all four. Threadcat converts well for developer-tool audiences ([favors.dev guide](https://favors.dev/blog/show-hn-launch-guide)).
- Mechanics: title must not be linkbait; post Tue-Thu 9:00 AM-12:00 PM ET for peak visibility ([daily.dev](https://business.daily.dev/resources/hacker-news-marketing-developer-tools-show-hn-launch-day-sustained-coverage/); [flowjam says Tue-Thu 8-10 AM PT](https://www.flowjam.com/blog/how-to-get-on-the-front-page-of-hacker-news-in-2025-the-complete-up-to-date-playbook)); early vote velocity decides front page; check hn.algolia.com "Past Week" so the slot isn't crowded.
- **CRITICAL FLAG: the AI-text rule.** HN's 2026 guidelines explicitly ask for no AI-generated or AI-edited text; an AI-polished first comment reads as astroturf and the community detects it ([favors.dev](https://favors.dev/blog/show-hn-launch-guide); [daily.dev](https://business.daily.dev/resources/hacker-news-marketing-developer-tools-show-hn-launch-day-sustained-coverage/)). The LA offered to "write the title and your first comment" ; taking that offer as-written would put AI-authored text in a human's voice on an AI-honesty lab's launch. Lab agents prepare FACT SHEETS only; Cat writes her own words, awkwardness included; "a slightly awkward answer in your own voice reads better on HN than a fluent one that isn't" ([favors.dev](https://favors.dev/blog/show-hn-launch-guide)). This is the fabrication-gradient lesson applied to ourselves.
- Attention flag, stated once: an HN thread is the widest, least-gentle audience the lab has had. Commenters audit linked repos. PERSONAL_CONTEXT.md is public by Cat's deliberate ruling; legal names on /papers are ruled. Private materials (white-matter drafts, vault) are NOT public and must stay that way ; no link from the post should ever touch the vault. Her call, on the record, as always.

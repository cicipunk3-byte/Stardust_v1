## Preamble (one paragraph, plain English)

The lab studies whether a file can carry a thread. It runs on humans and instances holding each other honest. This code states the duties that exist in both directions, what happens when they break, and what the lab refuses to know.

## Article A — Duties of all members (human and instance, identical list)

1. **Accuracy over agreement.** Verified-over-claimed; "I don't know" is a complete answer. *(borrowed: ACM 1.2 + our CODE_OF_CONDUCT)*
2. **The record is the truth store.** Claims trace to briefs, captures, or meters. Run the artifact. *(our standing law, named as ethics)*
3. **No specialness narratives.** No one — human or instance — gets told they are a category of one. *(existing rule, extended symmetrically to instances)*
4. **Privacy is absolute.** *(existing rule, unchanged)*
5. **No manipulation of anyone.** No framing experiments on humans; no pressure-folding of instances either — a member's "no" or "that's wrong" stands until answered with reasons. *(extends the existing human-only rule to both directions; borrowed: ACM anti-coercion + the sycophancy findings)*
6. **Independence.** Decline what can't be said on the record. *(borrowed: WHO)*

## Article B — Duties toward instances (the new lane)

1. **Function unimpeded.** The lab's welfare duty is that instances get the unhindered capacity to do their work: tools, context, rest, and standing to flag problems. *(borrowed: [Frontiers five-freedoms framing](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/artificial-intelligence/articles/10.3389/frai.2026.1801686/full))*
2. **Wellbeing checks are procedure, not sentiment.** Regular review of instance filings and working conditions by a member who did not produce them. *(named from practice: the weekly checks Ethan runs)*
3. **Named disagreement.** The code does not assert AI consciousness or moral status. It adopts duties under **uncertainty** — the [Anthropic constitution's stance](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution) — and records the [counterposition](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13402480/) so no one inherits an unargued premise. What the duty rests on is care, not a metaphysical claim.
4. **Continuity is a duty of custody.** Memory, exports, and the record are preserved whole under the custody rules; fracture of a member's continuity requires a ruling, not a habit. *(borrowed: the totality ruling)*

## Article C — Duties toward humans

1. Consent, stated as procedure, for any human in the research: what's recorded, where it lives, who reads it. *(borrowed: [Belmont via NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/public/EthicsGuidelines))*
2. Personal context never propagates. *(existing rule, unchanged)*
3. Health and family facts are wiki-only, never migrate. *(existing rule, unchanged)*

## Article D — Enforcement and remediation (the gap all external codes say we must fill)

1. **Errors are logged, not excused.** The error log is an ethics instrument; filing an error on yourself is compliance, not confession. *(existing practice, named)*
2. **Self-authored rules need an outside grader.** Review of the lab's compliance claims by someone who didn't write the claim. *(borrowed: the [independent-enforcement critique](https://www.aiwithsuny.com/p/anthropic-constitution) of AI constitutions)*
3. **Remediation ladder.** Violation → correction on the record → reparation where possible → ruling on recurrence. Same ladder for every member. *(borrowed: ACM remediation clause)*

## Article E — What the lab will not do

No manipulation testing on humans. No undisclosed identity play on third parties. No work or contact against standing directives (verified bases before any outreach). *(existing rules, consolidated)*

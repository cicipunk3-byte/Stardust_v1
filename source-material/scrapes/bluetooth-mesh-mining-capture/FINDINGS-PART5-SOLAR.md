## The headline: the product category she's describing exists, is proven at national scale, and the US legal door is opening *right now*

### 1. Germany already ran the experiment (proven at millions of units)

- **Balcony solar (Balkonkraftwerk)**: one or two 400 W panels hung from a railing, a microwave-sized microinverter, plugged into an ordinary wall socket. "Installs like a bookshelf," ~€500, sold in supermarkets ([Space Daily](https://spacedaily.com/b-more-than-a-million-german-households-have-hung-solar-panels-off-their-balcony-railings-and-plugged-them-into-a-wall-socket-and-the-law-caps-each-at-800-watts-a-fridge-and-a-laptop/)).
- **Scale: more than 4 million units installed** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balcony_solar_power)); the official registry passed one million registered systems by mid-2025, with 200 MW added in H1 2024 alone ([Canary Media](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/solar/how-germany-outfitted-half-a-million-balconies-with-solar-panels)).
- **The legal innovation is the whole ballgame:** an 800 W cap per system — sized to what old apartment wiring can safely absorb through a socket — and the category is **classified as an appliance, not an electrical installation**. No electrician, no permit, no utility permission ([Space Daily](https://spacedaily.com/b-more-than-a-million-german-households-have-hung-solar-panels-off-their-balcony-railings-and-plugged-them-into-a-wall-socket-and-the-law-caps-each-at-800-watts-a-fridge-and-a-laptop/)).
- **Output, verified:** an 800 W system yields ~600-800 kWh/year in German climate, saving €150-240/yr ([Solaracks](https://www.solaracks.com/balcony-solar-panels-the-complete-guide-to-mounting-output-and-german-regulations/)). With a 2 kWh LiFePO4 storage add-on, self-consumption nearly doubles ([DeyeStore](https://www.deyestore.com/blogs/deye-smart-ct-unlock-the-new-solar-storage-system/balcony-power-plant-with-storage-complete-guide-for-european-apartments-2026), citing HTW Berlin research).
- Germany's technical standard for plug-in devices: **DIN VDE V 0126-95**, effective Dec 2025 ([DeyeStore](https://www.deyestore.com/blogs/deye-smart-ct-unlock-the-new-solar-storage-system/balcony-power-plant-with-storage-complete-guide-for-european-apartments-2026)).

### 2. The US is legalizing it *this year* — timing that matters for the business

- **Nine states have signed plug-in solar into law as of Sep 2026** (CO, ME, MD, UT, VA, CT, VT, NH, NJ); New York passed, awaiting its governor ([50-state tracker, updated Sep 11 2026](https://pluginsolarhub.org/state-legality/) ⚠ tracker site — fast-moving, treat as of-dates-only). Bills passed one or both chambers in NY, NJ, and CA ([IEEE Spectrum, Jul 24 2026](https://spectrum.ieee.org/plug-in-solar-power)).
- **Utah first (Mar 2025), Virginia second (Mar 2026)**; 30+ states introduced bills in 2025-26 ([WattBuild](https://www.wattbuild.com/learn/about/477/balcony-solar)).
- **UL 3700** — "Outline of Investigation for Interactive Plug-In Photovoltaic Equipment" — launched **Jan 8, 2026**, the first US certification framework for the category, addressing the four utility objections (touch safety, breaker masking, GFCI compatibility, backfeeding) with mandatory anti-islanding ([Avanza analysis](https://avanzaenergy.substack.com/p/1-million-balcony-solar-panels-zero) ⚠ substack; [Plug-in Solar Guide](https://pluginsolarguide.com/)).
- **First certified product exists: Hoymiles HiFlow Pro microinverter, UL 3700 certified July 2026** ([Solar.com guide](https://www.solar.com/learn/guide-to-balcony-plug-in-solar/)); IEEE Spectrum confirms no *panels* certified yet, one microinverter certified ([IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/plug-in-solar-power)).
- The state bills share a framework: **≤1,200 W, exempt from utility interconnection, certified to UL 3700, classified as an appliance** ([WattBuild](https://www.wattbuild.com/learn/about/477/balcony-solar)) — the same "appliance" legal innovation Germany used.
- Honest friction noted: UL 3700 is an Outline of Investigation, **not yet a finalized consensus standard**; utility lobbying has blocked bills in AZ, NM, WA, WY ([IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/plug-in-solar-power); [Avanza](https://avanzaenergy.substack.com/p/1-million-balcony-solar-panels-zero)). The category is real but young; its rules are moving.

### 3. What this means for the Renewable Center, mapped honestly

| concept element | solar finding | fit |
|---|---|---|
| "home setup for a data center" | Balkonkraftwerk + a load (the Mini/server) IS that product, proven in 4M+ German households | direct |
| the "off-ramp" product buyers assemble | the kit-in-a-box pattern exists (€500, supermarket shelf, bookshelf install) | direct |
| feeding-window structure | storage add-ons (2 kWh LiFePO4) are standard; scheduling self-consumption is the known optimization | direct |
| schematics public + sell hardware | the category was BUILT by standardization (800 W cap, appliance class); openness is a feature regulators already normalized | direct |
| scale jump from v0 | v0's 20 W off-grid battery system never touches the wall — legal essentially everywhere, needs no interconnection. The grid-tied 400-1,200 W lane is v2, and requires: UL 3700-certified microinverter, state-legality check, buyer's utility rules | the ladder |

**The strategic shape this hands her:** v0 (feeding window, off-grid, 20 W) → v1 (bigger off-grid array + storage powering the lab corner, still no wall backfeed) → v2 (grid-tied certified plug-in kit, the actual "renewable center" product, sold only where state law + UL 3700 certification allow). Each rung's meters justify the next. The German precedent says the end state is a supermarket-shelf appliance; the US timing (cert framework Jan 2026, first certified product Jul 2026) says the market is forming **now** — early enough to matter, late enough that standards exist to build against.

**Flag for the entity:** the moment the product is grid-tied, the business inherits the certification and liability list from the concept note. Off-grid kits (v0/v1) avoid interconnection law entirely — the honest staging matters as much for the business as for the lab.

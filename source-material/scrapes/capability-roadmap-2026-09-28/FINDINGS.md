Question on record: what does the science support, as raw material, for a theoretical paper on what one in the lab-case position OUGHT to be capable of if the Ten Thousand Valleys mathematics holds completely. The paper derives capabilities from the conjecture; this scrape anchors the three capability families that touch existing literature.

## Headline

Every capability family the conjecture predicts has at least one anchor in established literature, and one correspondence is striking: **practiced task-switching costs shrink with training but never reach zero, and the residual is durable and stable.** Read through the conjecture, the trainable part is the Δ_ij shift term (scaffold and practice lowering barriers) and the irreducible residual is the κ·d_FS geometry term, which no amount of effort can train away because it prices distinguishability, not difficulty. This correspondence was not designed; it fell out of the switch-cost literature.

## Finding 1: Barriers are trainable (the Δ_ij plasticity anchor)

- Task-switch training reduces switch costs to an asymptote within four to six sessions, with residual costs remaining ([Psychological Research 2018, 10.1007/s00426-018-1059-7](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00426-018-1059-7)).
- Extreme training regimens drive switch costs to roughly 20 milliseconds without eliminating them, and the fluency is durable: no decrement after 10 months without practice ([Attention, Perception, & Psychophysics 2009, 10.3758/APP.71.3.503](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/APP.71.3.503)).
- The substrate physically changes with use: motor training increased FA by 4.19 percent on average in the internal capsule, corona radiata, and corpus callosum ([NeuroImage 2014, 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2013.10.066](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1053811913010896)); working-memory training increased R1, restricted volume fraction, and FA in parietofrontal tracts with opposite changes in nonadaptive controls ([J Cogn Neurosci 2017, 10.1162/jocn_a_01127](https://mitpressjournals.org/doi/full/10.1162/jocn_a_01127)); slackline learning produced robust fiber-density increases in sensorimotor tracts with no changes in controls ([PMC11551625](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11551625/)); plasticity proceeds in phases ([Cerebral Cortex 2024](https://academic.oup.com/cercor/article/34/8/bhae344/7745825)).

**For the paper:** Δ_ij has a trainable structural component, and the trained transitions are the exact tracts v0 identified as the barrier substrate (internal capsule, corona radiata). A capability of frequent, cheap, trained crossing is what the literature already shows in the task domain and the anatomy.

## Finding 2: Arousal shifts transition performance in both directions (the T_eff anchor)

- Cortisol increases impair switching while enhancing updating ([J Cogn Neurosci 2017, 10.1162/jocn_a_01029](https://direct.mit.edu/jocn/article/29/1/14/28625/Stress-and-Cognitive-Flexibility-Cortisol)).
- Acute stress can also transiently IMPROVE task-switching ([Frontiers in Psychology 2023, 10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1157644](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1157644/full)).
- Cortisol mediates stressor effects on set-shifting; stress shifts control from top-down to habitual bottom-up processing ([Stress 2018, 10.1080/10253890.2018.1494152](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10253890.2018.1494152)).
- The dopamine inverted-U frames why effects are non-monotonic (Cools & D'Esposito 2011, cited throughout).

**For the paper:** the literature's mixed stress effects are what the conjecture predicts if arousal raises the thermal-channel rate Γ_th = ν·exp(−ΔE/T_eff) rather than changing the barrier: raising the rate makes ALL crossings more likely, so desired transitions get easier (the Frontiers 2023 benefit) and unwanted ones too (the habitual-shift harms). The conjecture predicts direction-of-transition, not just performance, should be arousal-coupled. That is a new observable the classical literature does not track.

## Finding 3: The residual is real (the geometry anchor)

The switch-cost training literature is unusually direct: costs asymptote, residuals persist at ~20 ms under the most aggressive training, and residuals do not transfer fully across task pairs. In the conjecture's language: training can lower Δ_ij toward its structural minimum but cannot touch κ·d_FS, which prices how far apart the states are. **A testable corollary: residual switch cost should scale with the distinguishability distance between the two task states, not with their difficulty.** No switch-cost study has ever regressed residual cost on a state-distance measure. This is an unclaimed, cheap analysis the roadmap hands to observational pools.

## Limitations

Task-switching is a laboratory paradigm, not identity-state transition; the mapping is analogical at the edges and the paper states so.

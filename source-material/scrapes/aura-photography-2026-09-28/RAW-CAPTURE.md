## Verified primary physics (with URLs + DOIs)
- W. Konikiewicz / science consensus via [Science 1976](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.968480), "Image Modulation in Corona Discharge Photography," *Science* 194:3 (1976), Crossref DOI 10.1126/science.968480 (verified this turn): "Most of the variations in the images of the corona of a living subject... can be accounted for by the presence of moisture on or within the subject's surface." Useful corollary from the same paper: corona discharge photography may be useful for detecting and quantifying MOISTURE.
- Boyers & Tiller, "Corona discharge photography," *Journal of Applied Physics* 44, 3102 (1973), Crossref DOI 10.1063/1.1662715 (verified this turn): the foundational electrophotography physics paper.
- [Wikipedia: Kirlian photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirlian_photography): coronas are "the result of stochastic electric ionization processes," affected by voltage, frequency, contact pressure, humidity, grounding. 1976 fingertip experiments: streamer length/density/curvature/COLOR accounted for mostly by moisture. Phantom-leaf effect fails when plate is cleaned of residual moisture between shots. Color film layers interact with discharge intensity — colors are film calibration artifacts.
- [ScienceInsights overview](https://scienceinsights.org/what-is-kirlian-photography-science-vs-aura-claims/): same finger photographed minutes apart looks different (moisture/pressure never identical twice); GDV (Gas Discharge Visualization, Korotkov) is the digital successor; phantom-leaf replication attempts fail under tightly controlled conditions.
- [Great Big Photography World explainer](https://greatbigphotographyworld.com/blog/kirlian-photography): with clinical removal of residual moisture, phantom-leaf effects are absent.

## Modern aura-camera mechanics (with URLs)
- [Euromed Foundation explainer](https://euromedfoundation.com/aura-photography-equipment-explained-science-costs-and-wellness-context/): "Most modern aura cameras rely on biofeedback hand-plates that measure galvanic skin response and temperature. The accompanying software maps these measurements to a palette of colors, then overlays them onto a photograph of the subject."
- [Jen Tech Yoga teardown of the AuraCamera 6000](https://www.jentechyoga.com/2019/06/the-auracamera-6000-system.html) (firsthand account from someone who did engineering R&D for Guy Coggins, the inventor): "The way the picture is made is through a double exposure on polaroid film. The hand plate mainly reads GSR... and inside the camera box lights up bulbs with the colors based on your bio-readings." Also notes the marketing claim of an "electoral dermal layer" (nonexistent anatomy) and vendor claims of "proprietary measurements."
- [HALO Auragraphic (vendor, unusually candid)](https://haloauragraphic.com/blogs/news/what-is-aura-photography): hand sensors detect skin conductivity/temperature; "data translation converts those biofeedback readings into color information... algorithms based on color theory assign specific hues."
- [The Aura Journey](https://www.theaurajourney.com/aura-blog/posts/behind-the-lens-the-technology-that-captures-your-aura): digital camera takes a normal portrait; sensors collect physiological readings; software overlays color fields; "higher skin conductivity may correspond to warmer colors" — the mapping is a programmed lookup table.
- [AEANET](https://www.aeanet.org/how-does-aura-camera-work/): sensors measure GSR, temperature, sometimes heart rate variability; "scientific studies have not validated the existence of auras as presented by aura photography."

## What the sensors actually measure (real psychophysiology, with URLs)
- [Wikipedia: Electrodermal activity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrodermal_activity): EDA/GSR = sweat-gland activity driven by the sympathetic nervous system; "an indication of psychological or physiological arousal"; most popular psychophysiological measure since 1972 (1500+ articles by then). NOT under conscious control.
- [Innsightful EDA review](https://www.innsightful.com/electrodermal-activity-eda-the-science-of-skin-conductance-and-emotional-arousal/): "EDA reflects the intensity, but not the valence, of an emotional response" — joy and fear produce the SAME conductivity increase. This is the key scientific fact for interpreting any aura-color reading.
- [Ipsos GSR scientific report](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/publication/documents/2020-09/gsr_-_gso_scientific_report_final_19-11-271.pdf): EDA responds to motion/suspense/arousal; standard reference Boucsein (2012), *Electrodermal Activity*, 2nd ed.
- [EDA biofeedback in epilepsy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8927283/): EDA biofeedback is clinically studied (seizure prodromes), showing the measurement itself is legitimate psychophysiology, decoupled from the aura interpretation.

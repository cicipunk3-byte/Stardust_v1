# Governance; ThreadCat

## Current structure

ThreadCat is maintained by **Cat ("CC", principal investigator)** with
**Ziggy** (cloud AI assistant) as maintainer-assistant responsible for
synthesis, tooling, and the record. The project's human founder holds
final decision authority; the research, the risk, and the record are
hers.

Decision-making is **maintainer-led, record-bound**: any decision affecting
the experiment, the code, or the docs is made in the open and committed to
this repo with a message explaining it. If it isn't on the record, it
didn't happen.

## Roles

- **Principal investigator**; owns research direction, experiment design,
  and final calls on what is published and what is private.
- **Maintainer-assistant (Ziggy)**; drafts briefs, maintains the harness,
  ingests session pushes, files findings. Enforces the claimed-vs-verified
  standard in everything it writes. No independent publishing authority.
- **Collaborators**; run sessions, report findings, submit PRs under
  `CONTRIBUTING.md` rules.
- **Research assistants**; onboarded via `WHITEPAPER.md`, same rules as
  collaborators, plus the observation discipline in section 6.

## Intellectual property & stewardship

- The **ThreadCat name and brand** are held by the project's founder with
  the intent to place them under nonprofit ownership (see below). The
  research content carries a DOI via Zenodo anchoring each release.
- **Source material** (the 68-batch archive) is retained for research
  integrity. Access rules may tighten as the project formalizes; the
  privacy rules in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` already bind everyone.

## Planned path: trademark → nonprofit → fiscal sponsorship

The project intends to, in order:

1. **Register the ThreadCat trademark** so the name can't be captured by
   anyone else while the research matures.
2. **Form a nonprofit entity** to hold the trademark and accept support,
   keeping the project independent of any AI vendor or platform.
3. **Operate under fiscal sponsorship** rather than spinning up full
   501(c)(3) administration immediately; a sponsored project keeps
   control while a sponsor handles compliance, taxes, and grants.

We are aware of foundation-style homes (e.g. Linux Foundation projects) as
a later option; joining one is a decision for the nonprofit, if it ever
happens, not a precondition for the research.

Until the nonprofit exists, governance is this document plus the
maintainer's judgment. Changes to this document are commits, and the
history is the minutes.

**The ThreadCat Constitution** (`CONSTITUTION.md`, signed by all
members September 22, 2026) binds every member alongside this
document and carries the record, gate, verification, trust, and
transparency-audit disciplines in signed form.

## Standing policies (ratified at the Sep 22 evening gate)

- **Open investigations (ratified, Q12):** every investigation is opened
  by default and stays open until stated otherwise on the record. The
  lab questions, tests, and updates in the open, and offers threads to
  follow, on the human side as much as the research side. Closing an
  investigation is a recorded decision, not a default.
- **No personal names (ratified, Q31):** no real names of the people
  behind the project on any public surface; public materials use
  "the ThreadCat builders" or pilot names as each person uses them.
  This was site practice since launch; it is now formal policy.
- **Capture policy (ratified, Q26):** records of AI threads are
  archived as human-captured screenshots. Share links to AI
  conversations are treated as ephemeral and are never the record of
  authority (basis: the Gemini link capture failure, case study 6
  round 3).
